Evolve · Era 0 evolution and Era 1 village views · sprite atelier · offline edition

A bohemian pixel-art sprite kit

267 hand-drawn pieces (169 from batches 1–7, 53 from the Era 1 audit follow-up, 45 for the Era 0 evolution view) and the procedural generator that composes them into a primordial pool, a shoreline, buildings, people, terrain, weather and seasons. Plain JavaScript and Canvas 2D: no network, no framework, no web font. Open this file straight from disk.

01 · Style bible and palette

Master palette · ramps of 5 tones, light → dark, plus ink and spark.

02 · Hand-drawn pieces

Each piece is a text grid of semantic characters: 1–5 MAIN tones, a–d ACCENT, s–v skin, h–k hair, m–q metal, w–z wood, f g l r foliage, e ember, o ink, * spark. Wall modules are drawn pre-skewed for the left face.

03 · Contact sheet

04 · Simulator · Era 0 and village

Era 0 is drawn while race.species is 'protoplasm': a side-on pool for stages 0–3, the shoreline for 4–7, then the hand-over to the village. Tap the pool to spark RNA. In the village, drag to pan, tap a group of citizens to float their resource icon, tap a building for a +1 pop. One view pixel is one citizen pixel; buildings, terrain, decor and smoke are drawn at ×2.
